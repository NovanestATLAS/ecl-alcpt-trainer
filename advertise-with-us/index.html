About our platform

The trainer is free and open to every learner. There is no sign-up wall: people open the site, practice and learn. It is an independent project published by Novanest Atlas.

  • Grammar practice

    4,309 explained practice questions across grammar, vocabulary, idioms and phrasal verbs, each with a short explanation.

  • Vocabulary training

    Word and meaning practice in American English, with a lesson on ECL vocabulary.

  • Listening exercises

    5,037 recorded listening questions that learners can replay as often as they like.

  • Idioms and phrasal verbs

    Reference lessons and quizzes on the expressions that trip learners up.

  • Lessons and resources

    Grammar lessons, topic pages and guides on how the ECL and ALCPT work and how to prepare.

  • Timed practice tests

    Practice sessions against the clock, with answer explanations and a box for the questions a learner missed.

Our audience

The site is written for people who want to improve their English and, in many cases, prepare for an English proficiency test.

  • ECL and ALCPT examination candidates
  • English language learners
  • Students and professionals
  • People improving their English proficiency
  • Users looking for online learning tools
