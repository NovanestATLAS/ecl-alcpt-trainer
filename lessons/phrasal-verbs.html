309 phrasal verbs, with meanings and examples

A phrasal verb is a verb plus a small word such as up, off, out or into, with a new meaning: call off means cancel. Listen to the examples, hide the meanings to test yourself, or practice with flashcards and a quiz.

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPRSTWZ
  • separable The object can go in the middle, and a pronoun must: call the meeting off, call it off.
  • inseparable The object always goes after: look after the kids, look after them.
  • no object Nothing follows the verb: The truck broke down.
