Pronouns: personal, possessive, reflexive and indefinite

Pronouns replace nouns so that you don't repeat them. Their form depends on their job in the sentence: subject, object, owner, or the same person as the subject.

After prepositions, use object pronouns: "between you and me" (not between you and I).

Common mistakes

  • ✗Wrong: Me and him went to the PX.✓Right: He and I went to the PX.
  • ✗Wrong: This book is her's.✓Right: This book is hers.
  • ✗Wrong: The dog wagged it's tail.✓Right: The dog wagged its tail.

They take a singular verb: "Everyone is here." · "Nobody knows the answer." They and their can refer back to them: "Somebody left their jacket."

How the ECL and ALCPT test pronouns

Pronoun items usually give four forms of the same pronoun (they, them, their, theirs) and only one fits the job of the blank. Ask what the word does in the sentence: before the verb it is the subject; after a verb or a preposition it is the object; before a noun it is a possessive adjective; alone it is a possessive pronoun; and it is reflexive when it points back to the subject.

Practice itemAnswerWhy
The captain gave the maps to Lewis and ___.meAfter the preposition to you need an object pronoun. Test it by removing "Lewis and": you would say gave the maps to me, never to I.
The unit lost ___ radio during the exercise.itsIts (no apostrophe) is the possessive. It's always means it is or it has.
We painted the barracks ___; nobody helped us.ourselvesBy ourselves or just ourselves at the end means without help; the reflexive matches the subject we.
Some soldiers went to the range; ___ stayed in the barracks.the othersThe others means the rest of the same group. Others alone would mean some different, unknown people.

When two pronouns are joined by "and", test each one alone: "He and I went" (he went, I went); "for him and me" (for him, for me).

Tricky cases

These points come up again and again in grammar questions and in everyday speech.

  • Everybody, somebody, nobody, each take a singular verb: Everybody has a pass. Each of the drivers has a radio.
  • One / ones replace a noun you do not want to repeat: I don't like the blue shirt; I prefer the white one. These boots are too small; do you have bigger ones?
  • You and they can mean people in general: You need a pass to enter the base. They say the new gym opens next month.
  • It is an empty subject for weather, time and distance: It is raining. It takes two hours to drive there. It is ten miles to the airfield.

Common mistakes

  • ✗Wrong: Everybody have finished the test.✓Right: Everybody has finished the test.
  • ✗Wrong: Is raining again.✓Right: It is raining again.
  • ✗Wrong: My brother he is a pilot.✓Right: My brother is a pilot.
