Prepositions of time, place and movement, and fixed combinations

Prepositions are short words with fixed habits. Learn them in groups: time, place, movement, and the words that always take the same preposition.

Prepositions of time

PrepositionUseExamples
atclock times, noon, night, holidaysat 0800, at noon, at night, at Christmas
ondays and dateson Monday, on July 4th, on my birthday, on Friday morning, on the weekend
inmonths, years, seasons, parts of the day, long periodsin May, in 2026, in winter, in the morning, in the 1990s
fora length of timefor three hours, for ten years
sincea starting point (present perfect)since 2019, since Monday
duringwithin a period (+ noun)during the flight, during the storm
bynot later than (a deadline)Finish the report by Friday.
until, tillup to that time (the action continues)We waited until midnight.
in + a periodafter a period of time from nowThe bus leaves in ten minutes.

ECL tipIn time means early enough: "We arrived in time for the briefing." On time means at the planned time: "The train left on time."

Common mistakes

  • ✗Wrong: I'll see you in Monday.✓Right: I'll see you on Monday.
  • ✗Wrong: He has lived here since five years.✓Right: He has lived here for five years.

Prepositions of place

PrepositionUseExamples
ata point, an address, an eventat the gate, at 25 Main Street, at a meeting, at the airport
ininside a space, cities, countriesin the room, in the truck, in Texas, in Morocco
ona surface, a line, floors, streetson the table, on the wall, on the second floor, on Main Street
above, overhigher thanThe helicopter flew over the base.
below, underlower thanThe cat is under the bed.
between (two, or separate items) · among (a group)in a groupbetween the two trucks · among the soldiers
next to, beside, byat the side ofThe PX is next to the gym.
in front of, behind, across from (opposite)positionThe jeep is parked behind the building. · The PX is across from the bank.

Transportation: in a car or a taxi; on a bus, a train, a plane or a ship; by car, by bus; on foot.

Prepositions of movement

PrepositionExample
toWe drove to the airport.
into, out ofHe jumped into the water. · She ran out of the building.
onto, offThe cat jumped onto the roof. · Get off the bus at the next stop.
across: from one side to the other of a surfaceThey swam across the river.
through: inside something, from one end to the otherThe train went through the tunnel.
alongWe walked along the beach.
up, downHe climbed up the ladder.
toward, pastShe walked toward the gate. · We drove past the hospital.

Common mistakes

  • ✗Wrong: He arrived to the airport.✓Right: He arrived at the airport.
