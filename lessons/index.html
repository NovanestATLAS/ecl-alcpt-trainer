How to use the lessons

  1. Read and listen

    Read the rule cards and press the speaker button to hear each example in American English.

  2. Check yourself

    Answer the quick check at the end of the lesson. The explanation appears as soon as you answer.

  3. Practice in the trainer

    Each lesson links to the matching questions in the trainer, where your mistakes come back until you know them.

Hold the mouse over a word (or tap it on a phone) to see it in your language: the site uses the language of your device, and you can choose another one. The Translate switch at the top turns this on or off. Translation is there to check an answer, not to find it: in a Quick check, translate the words once you have answered. In the trainer, translation opens only after you answer, never in the timed tests.
