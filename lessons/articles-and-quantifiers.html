a/the

Grammar lesson 11 of 14 · 6 min read

Nouns, articles (a, an, the) and quantifiers (much, many, few, little)

Whether a noun is countable or uncountable decides which article and which quantifier can go with it. This lesson puts the rules together.

Countable and uncountable nouns

CountableUncountable
have a plural: a soldier, two soldiershave no plural: water, money, information
take a / an in the singular: a trucktake no a / an: some advice, a piece of advice
many, few, a few, a number ofmuch, little, a little, an amount of

Common uncountable nouns: advice, information, news, equipment, furniture, luggage, baggage, money, traffic, weather, work, homework, knowledge, research, evidence, damage, fuel, mail, clothing.

Common mistakes

  • ✗Wrong: He gave me an advice.✓Right: He gave me some advice. (or a piece of advice)
  • ✗Wrong: The news are good.✓Right: The news is good.
  • ✗Wrong: We need more equipments.✓Right: We need more equipment.

Plural rules

RuleExamples
most nouns: + sbook → books, truck → trucks
-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z: + esbus → buses, box → boxes, watch → watches
consonant + y: y → iescity → cities, army → armies (but day → days)
-f, -fe: → vesknife → knives, wife → wives, half → halves (but roof → roofs, chief → chiefs)
-o: + es or + spotato → potatoes, hero → heroes (but radio → radios, photo → photos)
irregularman → men, woman → women, child → children, foot → feet, tooth → teeth, mouse → mice, person → people
the same in the singular and pluralsheep, fish, deer, aircraft, series, species

Some nouns are always plural: pants, jeans, scissors, glasses, binoculars, clothes, police, people. "The police are coming."

A, an and the

ArticleWhenExample
a / anone of many, the first mention, a jobI saw a truck. · She is an engineer.
thesomething specific or already mentioned, the only oneThe truck was green. · the sun, the moon
theoceans, seas, rivers, deserts, mountain ranges, plural country names, ordinal numbers, superlativesthe Pacific, the Nile, the Sahara, the Alps, the United States, the first, the best
no articleplural and uncountable nouns in generalSoldiers need discipline.
no articlenames of people, cities, states, most countries, single mountains, lakes, languages, meals, sportsChicago, Mount Everest, Lake Michigan, English, breakfast, soccer
no articlego to bed / school / work / church / jail, for the usual purposeHe goes to school by bus.

Use an before a vowel sound, not a vowel letter: an hour, an MP, an honest man, but a uniform, a European country, a one-way street.

Common mistakes

  • ✗Wrong: She is engineer.✓Right: She is an engineer.
  • ✗Wrong: The life is beautiful.✓Right: Life is beautiful.

Quantifiers

With countable nounsWith uncountable nounsWith both
many questionsmuch timea lot of, lots of, plenty of
a few (some, enough)a little (some, enough)some (positive) · any (negative and questions)
few (not many: a negative idea)little (not much: a negative idea)no, none of, all, most
a number ofan amount of, a great deal ofmore, the most
each, every, either, neither + singular noun · both + plural noun
  • We have a little time, so let's get some coffee. (some time)
  • We have little time. We must hurry. (almost no time)
  • Few people came to the meeting. (disappointing)
  • Each soldier has his own locker. (singular verb)

Common mistakes

  • ✗Wrong: How much soldiers are there?✓Right: How many soldiers are there?
  • ✗Wrong: I have a few money.✓Right: I have a little money.

How the ECL and ALCPT test articles and quantifiers

These items test whether a noun is countable, whether it is specific, and how much of it there is. First decide: can you count it (one truck, two trucks) or not (information, equipment, advice)? Then ask whether the reader knows which one you mean.

Practice itemAnswerWhy
She is ___ honest person.anA or an depends on the first sound, not the letter: the h in honest is silent, so an. Compare a uniform (a "you" sound) and an hour.
We need ___ information about the convoy.someInformation is uncountable: no an, no plural. Use some, a lot of or a piece of.
Hurry up! We have ___ time left.littleLittle (without a) means almost none and goes with uncountable nouns. A little would mean some, enough.
___ sun rises in the east.TheThere is only one sun: one-of-a-kind things take the.

A few and a little are positive (some, enough); few and little are negative (almost none). Few and a few go with countable nouns; little and a little with uncountable ones.

Tricky cases

Most article errors come from a few habits that are easy to fix.

  • No article for things in general: Soldiers need discipline. Use the for a specific group: The soldiers in my unit are tired.
  • First mention a, second mention the: I saw a truck. The truck was green.
  • Some fixed expressions have no article: go to bed, at school, by car, at night, at work. American English says in the hospital.
  • Each and every take a singular noun and verb: Every soldier has a number. Each of the soldiers has a number.

Common mistakes

  • ✗Wrong: He wears an uniform.✓Right: He wears a uniform.
  • ✗Wrong: There are much soldiers here.✓Right: There are many soldiers here.
  • ✗Wrong: Every soldiers must sign in.✓Right: Every soldier must sign in.
