Military English vocabulary practice

Words from service life in their American usage: roles such as commanding officer and executive officer, orders, units and operations.

Learn the words first: ECL vocabulary by theme.

Try these 10 questions, then open the answer to check yourself. The trainer has all 20 questions on this topic, mixed in a new order each time, with your mistakes saved for review.

  1. The officer in charge of a ship is the ______.

    • Amayor
    • Bcommanding officer
    • Cchairman
    • Dprincipal
    Show the answer

    B. commanding officer

    Commanding officer = the officer in command of a ship or a unit.

  2. A written order describing a mission is an operations ______.

    • Aletter
    • Border
    • Cnotice
    • Dbill
    Show the answer

    B. order

    Operations order (opord).

  3. Soldiers live in ______ on a base.

    • Abarracks
    • Bcabins
    • Clodges
    • Dhostels
    Show the answer

    A. barracks

    Barracks = the buildings where soldiers live.

  4. Anti-submarine warfare is abbreviated ______.

    • AAsw
    • BAws
    • CSaw
    • DAsm
    Show the answer

    A. Asw

    ASW = anti-submarine warfare.

  5. The person who steers the ship is the ______.

    • Ahelmsman
    • Bgunner
    • Ccook
    • Dpurser
    Show the answer

    A. helmsman

    Helmsman = the person who steers a ship.

  1. A short meeting to give information is a ______.

    • Adebate
    • Bbriefing
    • Clecture
    • Dtrial
    Show the answer

    B. briefing

    Briefing.

  2. To leave a military post without permission is to ______.

    • Aretreat
    • Bdesert
    • Csurrender
    • Dwithdraw
    Show the answer

    B. desert

    Desert = leave military service without permission.

  3. Equipment and supplies are handled by ______.

    • Alogistics
    • Btactics
    • Cstrategy
    • Ddoctrine
    Show the answer

    A. logistics

    Logistics.

  4. The rules that govern the use of force are the rules of ______.

    • Acommand
    • Bengagement
    • Cconduct
    • Daction
    Show the answer

    B. engagement

    Rules of engagement.

  5. A ______ is a group of ships operating together.

    • Asquad
    • Btask force
    • Cplatoon
    • Dbattalion
    Show the answer

    B. task force

    Task force = a group of ships or units brought together for one mission.
