Free ECL and ALCPT practice trainer

Original practice questions written for this site, not ECL or ALCPT test items. This site is not affiliated with DLIELC or the Department of Defense.

Practice 4,309 original ECL and ALCPT questions in your browser, free and without an account. Every answer comes with a short explanation, the 5,037 listening questions are recorded in American English, and the questions you miss go to a mistake box so you can review them later. Your progress is saved in your own browser.

What you can practice

  • Listening: short statements, questions and answers, short dialogs, conversations and short talks, read by a man's and a woman's American voice, like the listening part of the tests.
  • Grammar: tenses, sentence structure, modals, conditionals, the passive, reported speech, articles and prepositions.
  • Vocabulary: words in context, collocations, word forms, confusable words and the American and military vocabulary used in the ECL and the ALCPT.
  • Idioms and phrasal verbs: common American expressions and 309 phrasal verbs, with their meaning.
  • Practice tests: 80 numbered ECL and ALCPT practice tests of 50 listening and 50 reading questions, timed tests of 20 to 100 questions, and a 64-question module test.

How the listening practice works

Each listening question shows its four answer choices and a Play button. By default the recording waits for you: read the choices, press Play, then answer. You can replay the recording, play it more slowly and show its text after you answer, which is the best way to understand what you missed. In Auto mode each recording plays by itself as soon as its question appears: you answer at your own pace, then press Next for the next recording.

The 80 ECL and ALCPT practice tests

The ECL and ALCPT practice tests follow the same plan every time: the listening part first, then the reading part with its own timer. For each listening question you get seven seconds to read the question and the choices, the recording plays once, and you have seventeen seconds to answer. The tests share the question bank without overlapping, so a listening question never comes back in another test, and the list of tests shows your best score for each one.

The mistake box

Every question you miss is kept with its explanation. Smart review brings it back after one day, then three days, then a week; four correct answers clear it. Starting each session with your mistakes is the fastest way to stop repeating them.

Where to start

Take the free ECL practice test or the ALCPT practice test to see where you stand. Then study one weak area at a time with the grammar lessons and the practice pages by topic. The ECL test guide explains the format and scoring, the study plan shows how to organize your weeks, and the test-taking strategies help you on the day.
