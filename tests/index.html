How to get the most from these tests

Start with a full test. The ALCPT practice test gives you a score for each part. Take it without preparing, in a quiet place, with the timer on. Write down your score and the date: it is your starting point.

Then work on one skill at a time. If listening cost you the most points, do the ECL listening practice and read the guide on how to practice listening. If grammar did, do the grammar practice and the practice page for the topic you missed most. For words, use the vocabulary practice and the vocabulary method.

Read every explanation, even when you were right. A right answer found by luck is a future mistake. The explanations tell you which word in the sentence decides the answer, so you learn to see it faster next time.

Measure your progress once a week. Take a longer timed test in the trainer at the same time each week. A practice percentage is not an ECL score, but if it rises week after week, your English is improving. Before the real test, read the test-taking strategies to avoid losing easy points.
