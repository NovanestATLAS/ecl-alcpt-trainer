Short statements 10 questions

You hear one sentence. Listen for numbers, times, places and the order of events.

  1. Listen, then answer.

    1When does the flight leave?

    What you heard

    The flight to San Antonio leaves at fourteen fifteen, not at four fifteen.

    B. At 2:15 in the afternoon In military time, fourteen fifteen (1415) is 2:15 p.m. The speaker corrects the mistake "four fifteen".
  2. Listen, then answer.

    2How many students passed the second test?

    What you heard

    Only fifteen students passed the first test, but fifty passed the second one.

    A. 50 Fifty (stress on FIF-) passed the second test. Fifteen (stress on -TEEN) passed the first one.
  3. Listen, then answer.

    3Why can't the man just walk in to get his flu shot?

    What you heard

    My flu shot is due, but the clinic's walk-in hours conflict with my shift, so I'll have to make an appointment.

    D. He is on duty during those hours. The walk-in hours conflict with his shift, so he is working at those times.
  4. Listen, then answer.

    4What is true about the new truck?

    What you heard

    The new truck is heavier than the old one, but it uses less fuel.

    D. It uses less fuel than the old truck. The new truck is heavier, but it uses less fuel.
  5. Listen, then answer.

    5When is the meeting now?

    What you heard

    The meeting has been moved from Tuesday to Thursday, at the same time.

    C. On Thursday "Moved from Tuesday to Thursday": the new day is Thursday, at the same time as before.
  6. Listen, then answer.

    6How many boxes of paper do they need?

    What you heard

    We need two boxes of paper for each classroom, and there are six classrooms.

    A. Twelve Two boxes for each of six classrooms: 2 x 6 = 12 boxes.
  7. Listen, then answer.

    7What will happen if it rains?

    What you heard

    If it rains tomorrow, the field exercise will start an hour later.

    A. The exercise will begin later. If it rains, the exercise still takes place, but it starts an hour later.
  8. Listen, then answer.

    8How much did the woman pay?

    What you heard

    The jacket was eighty dollars, but I got it for half price.

    B. $40 Half of eighty dollars is forty dollars.
  9. Listen, then answer.

    9At what time will the convoy reach the port?

    What you heard

    The convoy leaves the base at twenty-two hundred, and the drive to the port takes four hours.

    A. At 2:00 a.m. "Twenty-two hundred" (2200) is 10:00 p.m., and the drive "takes four hours". Four hours after 10:00 p.m. is 2:00 a.m.
  10. Listen, then answer.

    10What day is it today?

    What you heard

    The tires we ordered were delivered on Monday, the day before yesterday.

    D. Wednesday "The day before yesterday" was Monday, so yesterday was Tuesday. Today is Wednesday.

Questions and answers 7 questions

You hear a question. Choose the best answer to it.

  1. Listen, then answer.

    11Choose the best answer.

    What you heard

    How long have you been stationed at this base?

    A. Since last March. "How long" asks about a period of time. "Since last March" gives the start of that period.
  2. Listen, then answer.

    12Choose the best answer.

    What you heard

    Would you mind closing the window?

    D. Not at all. I'll do it now. "Would you mind...?" is a polite request. "Not at all" means "I don't mind": the speaker agrees to help.
  3. Listen, then answer.

    13Choose the best answer.

    What you heard

    Can I give you a hand with those boxes?

    B. Thanks, that would be great. "Give someone a hand" means help them. The best answer accepts the help.
  4. Listen, then answer.

    14Choose the best answer.

    What you heard

    How often do you have English class?

    C. Five days a week. "How often" asks about frequency: five days a week.
  5. Listen, then answer.

    15Choose the best answer.

    What you heard

    I can't make sense of my notes from today's lecture. Can I borrow yours?

    D. Sure, but mine aren't much better. He agrees to lend his notes but warns they may not help much.
  6. Listen, then answer.

    16Choose the best answer.

    What you heard

    How much is a round-trip ticket to Boston?

    D. It's ninety-five dollars. "How much" asks about a price. Only "ninety-five dollars" is a price.
  7. Listen, then answer.

    17Choose the best answer.

    What you heard

    Everyone's waiting for you at the restaurant. How soon can you get here?

    B. In about ten minutes. "How soon" asks how much time will pass before something happens. Only "In about ten minutes" answers that.

Short conversations 10 questions

You hear a man and a woman. Listen to the second speaker carefully: the answer is usually there.

  1. Listen, then answer.

    18What does the man mean?

    What you heard

    Woman: Are you coming to the briefing at zero eight hundred?

    Man: I wouldn't miss it for the world.

    D. He will certainly go to the briefing. "I wouldn't miss it for the world" means "I will certainly be there."
  2. Listen, then answer.

    19Where is the personnel office?

    What you heard

    Man: Excuse me, where can I get my ID card renewed?

    Woman: At the personnel office. It's on the second floor of Building Four, next to the post office.

    A. On the second floor of Building 4 The woman says "the second floor of Building 4, next to the post office".
  3. Listen, then answer.

    20Why did Sergeant Lopez miss physical training?

    What you heard

    Man: Why didn't Sergeant Lopez come to physical training this morning?

    Woman: He hurt his ankle playing basketball yesterday.

    B. He was injured. He hurt his ankle yesterday (an injury), so he could not train this morning. The basketball game was yesterday.
  4. Listen, then answer.

    21What does the man mean?

    What you heard

    Woman: How did the inspection go?

    Man: It was a piece of cake.

    A. The inspection was easy. "A piece of cake" means something very easy.
  5. Listen, then answer.

    22How will the man travel to Dallas?

    What you heard

    Woman: Are you driving to Dallas this weekend?

    Man: I was going to, but my car is in the shop, so I'll take the bus.

    A. By bus "I was going to (drive), but my car is in the shop" (being repaired), "so I'll take the bus".
  6. Listen, then answer.

    23What does the woman mean?

    What you heard

    Man: I think the new schedule is much better.

    Woman: I couldn't agree more.

    A. She completely agrees with the man. "I couldn't agree more" means "I agree completely."
  7. Listen, then answer.

    24Where is the flashlight?

    What you heard

    Man: Have you seen my flashlight?

    Woman: It's on the shelf, behind the first aid kit.

    C. Behind the first aid kit The woman says it is "on the shelf, behind the first aid kit".
  8. Listen, then answer.

    25What happened to the woman's request?

    What you heard

    Man: Did the captain approve your leave request?

    Woman: No, she turned it down.

    D. It was refused. To "turn down" a request means to refuse it.
  9. Listen, then answer.

    26What does the man mean?

    What you heard

    Woman: Daylight saving time ended last night. Did you set your watch back an hour?

    Man: Oh no. It completely slipped my mind.

    A. He forgot to change his watch. "It completely slipped my mind" means "I forgot all about it." He did not set his watch back.
  10. Listen, then answer.

    27What does the woman mean?

    What you heard

    Man: Did you catch your flight to Chicago last night?

    Woman: Yes, but I got to the gate in the nick of time.

    C. She almost missed her flight. "In the nick of time" means at the last possible moment. She caught her flight, but she almost missed it.

Longer conversations and announcements 3 questions

Longer recordings, as at the end of the listening part. Listen for the main point and for the details the question asks about.

  1. Listen, then answer.

    28At what time will the man pick the woman up?

    What you heard

    Woman: My flight gets in at nine forty tonight. Could you pick me up at the airport?

    Man: Sure, but I'm on duty until ten. Is ten thirty all right? I'll meet you outside baggage claim.

    Woman: That's fine. It always takes a while to get my bags anyway.

    C. At 10:30 p.m. The man is "on duty until ten", so he offers "ten thirty", and the woman says "That's fine." Nine forty is when her flight lands.
  2. Listen, then answer.

    29Which track will the Washington train leave from?

    What you heard

    Attention, please. The eleven forty train to Washington will leave from Track Six today, not from Track Two. Because of work on the tracks, the train is running about fifteen minutes behind schedule. We apologize for the delay.

    B. Track 6 The train "will leave from Track Six today, not from Track Two." Eleven forty is the time of the train, and fifteen minutes is the delay.
  3. Listen, then answer.

    30What is the main point of the message?

    What you heard

    Hi, this is Sergeant Delgado from the transportation office, calling about your move. The movers can't come on Thursday after all. They'll pick up your household goods on Friday morning instead, starting at eight. Please call me back today to confirm that Friday works for you.

    A. The pickup day has changed. The movers "can't come on Thursday after all" and will come "on Friday morning instead". The message gives a new day for the pickup.

How to improve your listening

Listening is one of the two skills the ECL measures, and half of the ALCPT. Five minutes of daily practice makes a difference: learn the sounds of fast American English, practice numbers and military time aloud, and repeat sentences right after you hear them.
