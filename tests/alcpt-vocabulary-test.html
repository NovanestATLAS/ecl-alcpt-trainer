Vocabulary 30 questions

Choose the meaning of the word in the sentence, or the word that completes it. Military words, everyday American English, phrasal verbs and idioms.

  1. 1“Force projection” means:

    A. deploying military power at a distance Force projection = deploying military power far from home.

  2. 2The faucet in the kitchen is leaking. A “faucet” is:

    A. a tap that controls water flow Faucet (US) = tap (UK).

  3. 3He works the graveyard shift at the harbor. The “graveyard shift” is:

    A. the late-night work shift Graveyard shift = work period during the night (e.g., midnight–8 a.m.).

  4. 4We will handle tag questions next week.

    A. deal with two-word verb to deal with = handle = treat = study or discuss

  5. 5The next test will be a piece of cake.

    D. easy A piece of cake = something very simple, easy to do

  6. 6We need to strengthen the final paragraphs of this report.

    D. beef up Beef up = to make something stronger. (adding more meat to a weak, watery soup or stew)

  7. 7Take the elevator to the fifth floor. An “elevator” is:

    A. a machine that carries people between floors Elevator (US) = lift (UK).

  8. 8The army mobilized within 48 hours. “Mobilized” means:

    A. assembled and prepared forces for action To mobilize = to prepare and organize troops for active service.

  9. 9“Reconnaissance in force” means:

    A. an attack made to obtain information Reconnaissance in force = an attack made to find out the enemy's strength and positions.

  10. 10“Chokepoint” means:

    A. a narrow passage that can be blocked Chokepoint = a narrow passage (a strait, a pass, a bridge) that is easy to block.

  11. 11We rented an apartment downtown. “Downtown” means:

    A. in the city center Downtown = the central business district of a city.

  12. 12We finished the test 30 minutes early.

    B. got through with two-word verb to get through with = to finish, complete, end, conclude something

  13. 13I am ashamed _____ myself.

    A. of fixed expression. Ashamed of. Opposite is proud of.

  14. 14“Deterrence” is:

    A. preventing action through fear of consequences Deterrence = preventing action through fear of the consequences.

  15. 15I told you a little white lie.

    A. I fibbed. A little white lie = a small lie that is not serious = a fib.

  16. 16We need to review chapters 3 and 4 today.

    D. go over Go over = review = look at = review quickly

  17. 17We exhausted our supply of sugar. Go buy some more.

    C. ran out of Run out of = to use up your supply of something

  18. 18The officer was court-martialed. “Court-martialed” means:

    A. tried by a military court Court-martial = a military trial for offenses against military law.

  19. 19I finally figured out the problem with the radio. “Figured out” means:

    A. understood / solved To figure out = to understand or find the solution.

  20. 20Follow the chain of command. The “chain of command” is:

    A. the official line of authority Chain of command = hierarchy through which orders pass.

  21. 21I'll call you on my cell. A “cell” here is:

    A. a mobile phone Cell (cell phone) = mobile phone.

  22. 22A “contingency plan” is:

    A. a plan for a possible future event Contingency plan = a plan ready for a possible future event.

  23. 23A “sortie” is:

    A. a single operational flight by one aircraft Sortie = one operational flight by a single aircraft.

  24. 24A “task force” is:

    A. a temporary grouping for a specific mission Task force = a temporary grouping of units for a specific mission.

  25. 25Can you give me a ride to the base? A “ride” is:

    A. a trip in a vehicle, as a passenger Give someone a ride (US) = give someone a lift (UK).

  26. 26A “beachhead” is:

    A. a position secured on a hostile shore Beachhead = a position secured on an enemy shore.

  27. 27The entire meal, including drinks and tip, amounted to $512.

    B. came to Amount to = come to (add up to a total): The bill came to $512.

  28. 28“Attrition” refers to:

    A. gradual reduction of strength through losses Attrition = the gradual wearing down of forces.

  29. 29She was really mad at her brother. Here, “mad” means:

    A. angry In American English, mad = angry.

  30. 30Which shirt did you finally choose?

    C. pick out Pick out = select = choose

How to learn the words you missed

Write each word you missed in a sentence of your own, say it aloud, and review it again after one day, three days and a week. The ECL vocabulary list groups 132 words by theme with flashcards and a quiz, and the phrasal verbs list has 155 more. In the trainer, every word you miss goes into your mistake box and comes back until you know it.
